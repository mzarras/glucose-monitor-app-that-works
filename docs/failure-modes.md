# Failure modes

HANDOFF §9 Phase 3 "failure-mode pass": for each way the phone, iOS or the person can break the
normal flow, what the app does, what the person sees, and the test that proves it. Services side:
the reading pipeline, the store, alerts, Trio and uploads (`docs/architecture.md`). The screens'
banners are Integrator B's (`App/Sources/ViewState/AppBanner.swift`, tested in `AppBannerTests`);
the banner names below are theirs.

Most proofs are composition-level hosted tests (`AppTests/FailureModeTests.swift` and friends) that
drive the real `LiveAppServices` with a scripted sensor, a production database in a temporary
directory, an injected clock and monotonic clock, and fake notification center, audio, AlarmKit and
Trio App Group. Package tests prove the parts below the app. "§11" marks what only a phone can show
(HANDOFF §11; `docs/setup.md`).

## Listen mode (D45, alongside the Dexcom app)

While listening, the Trio handoff and both uploaders are off in the services (`trio.offWhileListening`,
`*.upload.offWhileListening`): nothing is written to Trio's App Group and nothing is queued, so the
Trio and Uploads columns below are "off" throughout; Trio keeps its own CGM path. Alerts work as in
pairing mode. Banners and the listen-mode wording are Integrator B's (`ListenModeRules.failure`).
