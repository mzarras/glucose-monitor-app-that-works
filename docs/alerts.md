# Alerts

How Sensor raises alerts, why it does it this way, and where each safety rule is enforced.
Code: `Packages/AlertEngine`. Sounds: `Resources/Sounds` (generated by `tools/sounds/generate_sounds.py`).
Scope: HANDOFF §4.7, §5, §7.3 item 3; PROGRESS.md D4, D11, D17, D18, D22, D26, D27; WS4 safety review (B1, M1, minors).

---

## 1. What makes an alert audible on iOS

| Mechanism | Silent switch on | Focus / DND | App must be running | Needs Apple approval |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Notification, `.active` / `.timeSensitive` | **vibrates only** | Time Sensitive breaks through if the user allows it | no | Time Sensitive: capability only (we have it) |
| Notification, `.critical` + `criticalSoundNamed` | plays, at the volume we set | plays | no | **yes**: `com.apple.developer.usernotifications.critical-alerts`, granted per team |
| In-app audio, `AVAudioSession` category `.playback` | plays, at the **media** volume | plays | **yes** (awake) | no; needs `UIBackgroundModes` `audio` to activate from the background |
| AlarmKit alarm (iOS 26+) | plays (system alarm) | plays | no | no; needs user authorization + `NSAlarmKitUsageDescription`. Used for urgent low (D26, §4.4) |

Consequences, which decide the design (PROGRESS.md D17):

- Time Sensitive alone is **not** audible on silent.
- Critical Alerts are the only notification-based way through the switch, and Apple rarely grants
  them to build-from-source apps. We request them anyway (D4) and use them automatically if granted.
- The G7 wakes the app over BLE roughly every 5 minutes, and every reading-driven alert fires during
  such a wake. So in-app `.playback` audio at that moment is the primary ringer-off path.
- An alert that fires while **nothing** wakes the app (missed readings, i.e. signal loss) can only be
  a pending notification, which is vibrate-only on silent unless Critical Alerts are granted. See §4.2.
- On iOS 26+, with the user's permission, urgent low and its repeats are also system alarms, which
  ring on silent while the app is suspended (D26, §4.4).

## 3. Rules (HANDOFF §5)

Defaults and ranges live in `AlertLimits.swift`; settings clamp on every path (initialiser, setter, decoding).

| Alert (`AlertKind`) | Condition | Default | Configurable | Sound | Sound on silent |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Urgent low `urgentLow` | ≤ threshold, or LOW flag | 55 mg/dL, on | threshold 50–60, sound, repeat 2–5 min (never off, never > 5), snooze 1–30 (default 30) | Cascade | **always** (+ system alarm on iOS 26+, §4.4) |
| Urgent low soon `urgentLowSoon` | value + reported rate × 20 min ≤ urgent-low threshold | off | on/off, sound, snooze | Tide | on |
| Low `low` | ≤ threshold, or LOW flag | 70, on | on/off, threshold 60–100, sound, snooze (30) | Dusk | on |
| High `high` | ≥ threshold, or HIGH flag | 250, on, no delay | on/off, threshold 120–400, sound, snooze (120), delay 0–120 min | Summit | on |
| Falling fast `fallingFast` | reported rate ≤ −threshold | 3 mg/dL/min, off | on/off, threshold 2–5, sound, snooze (30) | Swoop | off |
| Rising fast `risingFast` | reported rate ≥ +threshold | 3 mg/dL/min, off | same | Swoop | off |
| Missed readings `missedReadings` | no valid reading for the window | 15 min, on | on/off, window 10–60, sound, snooze (30) | Sonar | on (effective only while awake, §4.2) |
| Sensor warm-up done / expiring 24 h / expiring 2 h / grace period | one-off announcements | on | on/off each, sound | Doorbell | off |
| Sensor failed / error / expired | settled sensor state | on | on/off each, sound, snooze (120) | Warble | on |
| Bluetooth unavailable `bluetoothUnavailable` | `.bluetoothUnavailable(.poweredOff/.unauthorized/.unsupported)` | on | on/off, sound, snooze (30) | Warble | on |
| Trio bridge `trioBridge` | `TrioBridgeStatus.writeFailing` (`.notConsumedSince` ≥ 15 min exists but is not fed: not detectable, D22) | on | on/off, sound, snooze (30) | Warble | on |

Notes:

- **LOW/HIGH (S4).** `.belowRange` meets every at-or-below threshold and clears every at-or-above one;
  `.aboveRange` the reverse (`GlucoseConditions.atOrBelow/atOrAbove`). Neither is ever converted to 40/400;
  text writes "LOW (below sensor range)" / "HIGH (above sensor range)".
- **Urgent low soon (S1).** Uses only `GlucoseReading.trendRate` as reported. No rate → no projection
  (the condition is "unknown", nothing fires or clears). The projected number exists only inside the
  comparison; the notification shows the measured value, the reported rate, and the rule.
- **Supersession.** While urgent low is firing or snoozed, low and urgent-low-soon are cleared, so the
  two never sound together (as Loop does).
- **Hysteresis.** A glucose alert clears only 5 mg/dL past its threshold (rate alerts: 1 mg/dL/min).
- **Sensor states.** Only settled states (not paired, warming up, active, grace, expired, failed,
  error) affect sensor alerts. The 5-minute connection cycle (searching → connecting → authenticating)
  and Bluetooth states neither fire nor clear them. Bluetooth `.resetting` waits for the next state.
- **Missed readings** is suppressed while not paired or warming up, and its window restarts when the
  sensor leaves those states.
- **Announcements** (warm-up done, expiring, grace) fire once per sensor session. Session dates are
  derived from the phone clock at each connection (S3) and jitter by a second or so, so a date within
  an hour of the one already announced for that kind is the same session (review M1).
- **Quiet hours** (daily window, local time, wraps midnight): every alert except urgent low is posted
  `.passive` with no sound and no in-app sound, including low and missed readings (D27, exactly as
  §5). The decision is made for each pending notification's own fire time. Urgent low is never
  silenced (S7).

## 4. Delivery

### 4.1 Paths (`DeliveryPolicy.decide`, pure, fully tested)

| Situation | Notification | In-app `.playback` sound | System alarm |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quiet hours (not urgent low) | `.passive`, no sound | no | no |
| Sound on silent, Critical Alerts granted | `.critical`, `criticalSoundNamed(file, 1.0)` | no (the system plays it) | no (Critical wins, as in Loop) |
| **Urgent low**, alarms allowed (iOS 26+), Critical not granted — now or pending | `.timeSensitive`, `named(file)` | no (would clash) | **yes**, `alert-<sound>.caf` |
| Sound on silent, not granted, app awake | `.timeSensitive`, `named(file)` | **yes** (urgent low loops 30 s; others play once) | no |
| Sound on silent, not granted, pending (app may be suspended) | `.timeSensitive`, `named(file)` | not possible | no |
| Sound on silent off | `.timeSensitive`, `named(file)` (obeys the switch) | no | no |

The notification keeps its sound even when the app also plays it: with the ringer on but media
volume at zero, the notification sound is the one heard. With both up, both play (Loop accepts the
same). xDrip instead drops the notification sound when it plays in-app, which is silent in that case.

Urgent low is always treated as sound-on-silent and never silenced, in the engine **and** again in
`DeliveryPolicy` (`test_S7_urgentLowAlwaysGetsTheLoudestAvailablePath` enumerates every combination).

The in-app player vibrates at start and every 2 s while looping (Loop's pattern), so a phone with
media volume at zero still buzzes. A less important alert never interrupts a more important one's
sound (`AlertKind.priority`, urgent low first); cancelling an alert stops only its own sound.

### 4.2 The suspended-app gap (accepted, D17)

Missed readings and signal loss fire precisely when no BLE event wakes the app, so the in-app path
cannot run. The pending Time Sensitive notification is posted on time but, on a phone set to silent,
**vibrates without sound** (unless Critical Alerts are granted). Michael accepted this (D17). If the
app happens to be awake when it becomes due (foreground, or another wake-up), the engine emits
`playDueAlert` and the sound plays.

Urgent-low repeats between BLE wakes: on iOS 26+ with alarms allowed, each pending repeat is also a
system alarm and rings on silent (D26, §4.4; unproven on hardware). Otherwise they have the same
property as missed readings, and the next reading (≤ 5 min) wakes the app and plays the sound again.

No keep-alive technique is built (e.g. a silent looping audio session): that was ruled out.

### 4.3 Volume

`.playback` audio follows the media volume. iOS has no public API to set it. Loop and the Trio
worktree push it up by moving the hidden `UISlider` inside an `MPVolumeView`; by Loop's own comment
this no-ops when the app is backgrounded (the 3 a.m. case), and it depends on a private view
hierarchy. Not proven for our case, so not built. Instead `AlertDeliveryStatus.problems` reports
`.alarmVolumeMuted` (≤ 0.001) and `.alarmVolumeLow` (< 0.25) from `AVAudioSession.outputVolume`
for the Settings screen (S5). Critical Alerts, if granted, play at the volume we set regardless.

### 4.4 System alarms for urgent low (D26)

On iOS 26+ (deployment target stays 17.0), once the user allows alarms, urgent low rings as an
AlarmKit system alarm: on silent, in Focus, while the app is suspended, without the Critical Alerts
entitlement. Only urgent low uses alarms; every other alert is unchanged.

- **Policy.** `DeliveryPolicy` returns `systemAlarmSoundFileName` for urgent low when alarms are
  authorized and Critical Alerts are not granted (Critical wins, as in Loop `AlertManager.swift:451-457`).
  The Time Sensitive notification is still posted (visual, Snooze/Acknowledge, fallback sound); the
  in-app sound is not played (it would clash with the alarm).
- **Immediate alarm.** Scheduled 2 s ahead: a fixed alarm at or before now never rings (Loop
  `CriticalAlertAlarmScheduler.swift:73-76`, commit `25f79bd`).
- **Repeats while suspended.** Loop schedules only the immediate alarm, while the app is awake. Here
  every pending urgent-low repeat (the same slots as the pending notifications, 60 minutes ahead) is
  also an alarm, so the repeats ring while the app sleeps. **This is unproven on hardware** (whether
  a pre-scheduled `.fixed` alarm rings reliably, how a ringing alarm interacts with the next one, and
  AlarmKit's alarm limit — `AlarmManager.AlarmError.maximumLimitReached` exists but its value is not
  documented). It is a HANDOFF §11 check (§10 below).
- **IDs.** Fixed per slot (`SystemAlarmIDs`: slot 0 = the immediate alarm, 1…30 = repeats), like the
  notification identifiers. Nothing needs to be stored to find an alarm again after a relaunch; the
  snapshot therefore holds no alarm IDs (the review asked for persisted IDs; fixed IDs make them
  unnecessary). Rescheduling repeats cancels only the repeat IDs, never slot 0.
- **Cancel.** Acknowledge, snooze, recovery, supersession, and turning the alert off all emit
  `.cancel(.urgentLow)`: the executor stops and cancels **every** alarm the app has (`cancelAll`).
- **Launch reconcile.** `AlertService.start()` sends `.launched`: if urgent low is not firing,
  leftover pending notifications and alarms are removed; if it is firing, they are rebuilt from the
  restored state.
- **Failure.** Scheduling is async and can throw. The immediate alarm failing falls back at once to
  the D17 path (notification sound + in-app sound); a failed repeat leaves its pending notification.
  Either way `AlertDeliveryStatus.problems` gets `.urgentLowAlarmFailed(error)` until a later attempt
  succeeds in full (S5).
- **Stop.** The alarm's Stop button runs the app's `StopUrgentLowAlarmIntent`, which calls
  `UrgentLowAlarmIntentBridge.shared.stopTapped()` → `.acknowledge(.urgentLow)` → snooze for the
  urgent-low snooze time, ≤ 30 minutes (S7), and every alarm is cancelled. Taps that arrive before
  the service is connected are queued. A Snooze button, if added, goes through `snoozeTapped(minutes:)`
  and is clamped by the engine to ≤ 30.
- **Permission.** `SystemAlarmAuthorization` (`unavailable` below iOS 26, `notDetermined`, `denied`,
  `authorized`) is `AlertDeliveryStatus.urgentLowAlarm`, for the Settings "Urgent low alarm" row;
  `notDetermined` and `denied` are `DeliveryProblem`s. Granting it rebuilds the pending repeats as alarms.
- **Sound.** AlarmKit plays `alert-<sound>.caf` (IMA4), the same sound as the `.wav`: Loop verified on
  hardware that AlarmKit plays bundled IMA4 `.caf` files by name (PR #2520); WAV is unverified there.
  `test_alarmVariantsAreIMA4CAFsThatDecodeToTheSameSound` decodes each `.caf` with Apple's decoder.

### 4.5 What needs the Apple entitlement

Only `.critical` delivery. Without it: authorization still includes `.criticalAlert` (harmless),
`criticalAlertSetting` stays `.notSupported`, and the policy falls back to Time Sensitive + in-app
sound. To enable: Apple grants the entitlement to the team, then set `SENSOR_APP_ENTITLEMENTS =
App/Sensor+CriticalAlerts.entitlements` (D4). The app switches automatically at the next permission
check (`AlertService.refreshDeliveryStatus`), and pending notifications are rebuilt for the new level.

### 4.6 Notifications

- Identifiers: `sensor.alert.<kind>` (immediate; a new one replaces the old) and
  `sensor.alert.<kind>.slot<0…29>` (pending). Urgent low keeps 60 minutes of repeats pending (12 at the
  default 5 minutes, 30 at the 2-minute floor); missed readings keeps 12 (≥ 2 hours). At most 42
  pending, under iOS's 64. Pending notifications use one-shot interval triggers, never past times.
- Categories: `sensor.alert.category.snoozable` (Acknowledge, Snooze) and
  `sensor.alert.category.informational` (announcements); both `.customDismissAction`.
- Responses (`AlertNotificationResponse`): open or Acknowledge → acknowledge; Snooze → snooze for the
  duration carried in the notification (urgent low clamped to 30); swipe → stop the sound only (an
  accidental swipe must not end urgent-low repeats).

## 5. State machine

One per condition alert (`AlertEpisode`):

```
          condition met                      delay elapsed (high only)
 armed ────────────────▶ [delaying] ───────────────────────────────▶ firing
   ▲  ◀── clear ──────────┘                                           │   │
   │                                                     acknowledge /│   │ repeat slot or
   │                                                     snooze       ▼   │ re-alert interval
   │                                                              snoozed │ (sounds again)
   │                                                                │     │
   │               snooze over and condition still met ─────────────┘─▶ firing
   │
   └──────────── clear (recovered past the margin, turned off, superseded) ── from firing / snoozed
                 = "cleared": removes delivered + pending notifications, stops the sound
```

- **Condition** is `met`, `holding` or `clear`. `holding` means unknown or in the hysteresis band:
  no reading is current (older than 6 minutes, S1), no reported rate (urgent low soon, rates), a
  transient sensor state, Bluetooth resetting, or Trio not consuming for < 15 min. Holding never fires
  and never clears.
- **Delay (high)**: counts from the first reading at or above the threshold. A reading in the band or
  missing data does not restart it; only a recovered reading does (Loop's `decideAndUpdate`). It then
  fires on the first current reading at or above the threshold once the delay has passed.
- **Fire**: posts the notification now. Urgent low also schedules 60 minutes of repeats (notifications, and alarms on iOS 26+).
- **Urgent-low repeats**: every `repeatMinutes` (5) from the first sounding until acknowledged, snoozed
  or cleared — **including while the latest value is stale or in the band**. The alert was raised on a
  real reading and nobody acknowledged it; the pending notifications repeat it while the app sleeps,
  and each wake adds the ringer-off sound (`playDueAlert`) and rolls the pending ones forward. A wake up
  to 60 s before a slot counts as that slot (the G7 wakes about every 5 minutes, anchored at the wake
  that fired the alert, so the next wake often lands just before the slot). Repeat
  text always states the measured value and its time ("Not acknowledged. Last reading 49 mg/dL at
  12:05 PM.") so an old value is never presented as current (S1).
- **Other alerts** re-alert at their snooze interval while unacknowledged, but only when a *current*
  reading (or state) still meets the condition.
- **Acknowledge** = snooze for the alert's snooze minutes. **Snooze** applies only to a firing or
  snoozed alert: there is no pre-emptive snooze, so every new episode sounds. Both remove the alert's
  notifications and stop its sound.
- **Snooze expiry** re-fires only on a current reading/state that meets the condition. A stale value
  never re-fires anything (S1); missed readings covers the gap.
- **Clear** (recovery, supersession, alert turned off) re-arms and removes everything. A snooze never
  survives an episode.
- **Missed readings** has its own machine (`AlertEngine+MissedReadings.swift`): armed with its
  notifications pending from `last valid reading + window`; `firing` from that time; repeats every
  window; acknowledge reschedules from the snooze end; any valid reading (backfill included) clears it
  and reschedules. Refused readings (S2/S6) change nothing, including this clock.
- **Readings.** Glucose alerts evaluate only the newest *live, non-backfilled* reading, and only while
  it is ≤ 6 minutes old. A backfilled reading never fires, clears or re-decides a glucose alert (Loop's
  G7 bug, and the orchestrator's rule); it does advance the missed-readings clock. A live reading older
  than one already seen is ignored.
- **Relaunch.** `AlertEngineSnapshot` (Codable) holds every episode and fact; the app persists it
  after each input and restores it, so acknowledgements and snoozes survive a background relaunch.
  Decoding is field by field: a new, missing or unreadable field takes its default and an unreadable
  episode is dropped, so a format change never resets the engine. `.launched` reconciles what the
  previous process left pending.
- **Clock faults (review B1).** Every date the engine keeps is something that already happened. Before
  every input, and on restore, anything later than `now + 60 s` (`LiveReadingGate.futureTolerance`) is
  repaired: a future-dated latest reading is dropped (it would hide every real reading after a clock
  rollback), the missed-readings window restarts from now, firing/delay timers restart from now, a
  non-urgent snooze keeps its granted length from now, and an **urgent-low snooze ends at once** —
  how much real time has passed is unknowable once the clock has jumped back, so restarting the 30
  minutes could stretch it (S7). `currentReading` also refuses a reading more than 60 s in the future.
  A clock moving forward needs no repair: readings go stale (not evaluated), missed readings fires,
  snoozes end early, and a firing urgent low repeats once and reschedules.

## 7. Other safety rules

- **S1**: glucose alerts only on the current live reading (`AlertEngine.currentReading`, `accept`);
  urgent low soon only with a reported rate; text always carries the measurement time; no projected
  value shown; a reading dated in the future is not current and is dropped. Tests `test_S1_*` in
  `GlucoseAlertTests`, `AlertConditionsTests`, `AlertTextTests`, `ClockFaultTests`, including
  `test_S1_staleBackfillDoesNotFireGlucoseAlert`, `test_S1_futureDatedReadingIsNotCurrentAfterClockRollback`,
  `test_S1_urgentLowFiresAfterClockRollback`.
- **S2/S6**: every reading goes through `LiveReadingGate` inside `AlertEngine.handle`; a refused
  reading returns only `.readingRejected` and changes nothing. Release builds call only
  `validate` (sensor only); `validateAllowingReplay` is compiled only under `#if DEBUG` and used only
  when `AlertEngineConfiguration.acceptsReplayReadings` (a DEBUG-only property) is on (D11). Tests
  `test_S6_*`, `test_S2_implausibleReadingsNeitherFireNorClear`; the Release variant
  `test_S6_releaseBuildRefusesReplayReadings` runs under `swift test -c release -Xswiftc -enable-testing`.
- **S4**: `GlucoseConditions`, `AlertText.valueText`; `test_S4_*`.
- **S5**: missed readings, Bluetooth, sensor failed/error/expired, Trio bridge are alerts; denied
  notifications, muted/low media volume, alarms not allowed and alarm scheduling failures are
  `DeliveryProblem`s for Settings; missed readings survives a clock rollback. `test_S5_*`, including
  `test_S5_alarmSchedulingFailureFallsBackAndSurfaces`, `test_S5_missedReadingsSurvivesClockRollback`.
  Permissions (notifications, Critical, alarms) are re-read on every input, so the wake that delivers
  an urgent low uses the current delivery level.

## 8. Sounds

Eight original sounds, synthesised from arithmetic by `tools/sounds/generate_sounds.py` (Python
standard library only, deterministic; `--check` verifies the committed bytes). Each as 16-bit mono
22.05 kHz WAV (notifications, in-app sound) and IMA4 CAF (AlarmKit, §4.4), 2.4–5.2 s. GPL-3.0, like
the rest of the repository. Provenance and checksums:
`Resources/Sounds/README.md`. They differ in rhythm and pitch contour, not just pitch; none is a
constant-pitch train of high square-wave beeps (the classic pager tone). Verify by ear on device
(HANDOFF §11 step 1).

| Sound | File | Character | Default for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cascade | `alert-cascade.wav` | fast falling four-note bursts, repeated | urgent low |
| Tide | `alert-tide.wav` | slow falling two-note swell, twice | urgent low soon |
| Dusk | `alert-dusk.wav` | three soft descending wooden notes | low |
| Summit | `alert-summit.wav` | three bright ascending bell notes | high |
| Swoop | `alert-swoop.wav` | rising-then-falling glide, twice | falling fast, rising fast |
| Sonar | `alert-sonar.wav` | single ping with echo, repeated once | missed readings |
| Doorbell | `alert-doorbell.wav` | two-note ding-dong | sensor announcements |
| Warble | `alert-warble.wav` | low two-tone trill in three bursts | sensor failed/error/expired, Bluetooth, Trio bridge |

The picker's preview is `AlertService.previewSound(_:)`; it never interrupts an alert's sound.

## 9. Wiring notes for Phase 2 (App target)

- Create one `AlertService` at launch with `UserNotificationCenterAdapter()`,
  `PlaybackAlertAudioPlayer()`, `SystemAlertClock()`, the persisted `AlertSettings`
  (`AlertSettings.decodeOrDefault`) and `AlertEngineSnapshot.decode(...)`; persist `onSnapshot` output
  (App Group, `completeUntilFirstUserAuthentication`). Call `start()`.
- Feed `.reading(r, session:)` **only** for readings returned by `GlucoseReadingRepository.insert`
  (newly stored, live). Never feed `latestLiveReading()` results, history or imported data.
- Feed every `CGMSource.state` value as `.sensorState`, TrioBridge status as `.trioBridge`, and `.tick`
  on every wake and about once a minute in the foreground.
- `UNUserNotificationCenterDelegate.didReceive`: `service.handleNotificationResponse(
  UserNotificationCenterAdapter.responseKind(actionIdentifier:), userInfo:)`. `willPresent`: present
  banner + sound + list.
- Onboarding: `requestAuthorization()`, then (iOS 26+) `requestUrgentLowAlarmAuthorization()` with an
  explanation screen. Settings and app foreground: `refreshDeliveryStatus()`, show `problems`, and an
  "Urgent low alarm" row from `deliveryStatus.urgentLowAlarm`. DEBUG replay mode sets
  `AlertEngineConfiguration.acceptsReplayReadings = true`.
- System alarms (D26): pass `systemAlarms: SystemAlarmAdapter(makeStopIntent: { StopUrgentLowAlarmIntent() })`
  to `AlertService` (on iOS below 26 it reports `.unavailable` and nothing changes). Add the intent to
  the **app target** (App Intents metadata is extracted from the app's own sources); the code is in
  `UrgentLowAlarmIntentBridge.swift`'s header comment. Connect the bridge once the service exists:
  `await UrgentLowAlarmIntentBridge.shared.connect { await service.send(.user($0)) }`.
  `NSAlarmKitUsageDescription` is already in `App/Info.plist` (D26).
- The `.caf` and `.wav` files are both copied flat into the bundle root (`Resources/Sounds`).

## 10. On-device checks (HANDOFF §11)

The host tests prove the decisions; these need the phone:

1. Ringer off, app backgrounded, BLE wake with an urgent low: the in-app sound plays (D17 path, AlarmKit off).
2. iOS 26+, alarms allowed: urgent low rings as a system alarm playing Cascade; **the pre-scheduled
   repeat alarms ring 5 and 10 minutes later with the app suspended** (unproven: Loop only schedules
   immediate alarms); Stop acknowledges (the next repeat does not ring; notifications are removed).
3. How many alarms AlarmKit accepts (30 at the 2-minute repeat floor); a refusal shows the
   "urgent low alarm failed" problem in Settings.
4. A phone call during the urgent-low loop: the sound resumes when the call ends.
5. Set the clock back and forward by hand with an urgent low in progress (review B1 scenarios).
6. Media volume at zero: Settings shows the warning; the notification sound still plays with the ringer on.
