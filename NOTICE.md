## Sounds

The alert sounds in `Resources/Sounds` are original, generated by `tools/sounds/generate_sounds.py`
(GPL-3.0). The IMA ADPCM encoder uses the step and index tables from the public IMA "Recommended
Practices for Enhancing Digital Audio Compatibility in Multimedia Systems" (1992).
